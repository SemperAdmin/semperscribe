# The NJP paper package, a clerk's map

Research only. Nothing here is built. This maps the documents surrounding NAVMC 10132 for a
Marine Corps administrative clerk at battalion or squadron level: what exists before the Unit
Punishment Book is opened, what runs alongside it, and what the clerk owns after the commanding
officer imposes.

Scope is enlisted Marine Corps NJP. Officer cases are recorded in section 9 and are out of app
scope by product decision, not by regulation. See spec decision D-14.

Confidence is marked throughout. CONFIRMED means read from primary text. REPORTED means a single
source with no independent cross-check. UNCONFIRMED means it could not be pinned down, and the
source that would settle it is named.

---

## 0. Read this first. Three of your sources are lying to you.

Every one of these was verified during this research, and each one will hand a clerk the wrong
answer today.

### 0.1 The MCO 5800.16 volumes posted on marines.mil are unamended (CONFIRMED)

MARADMIN 427/23, of 281800Z AUG 23, amended both volumes. The public PDFs do not reflect it.

Volume 14 still carries paragraphs 011105.A through 011105.R, the detailed item-by-item
preparation instructions. MARADMIN 427/23 deleted all eighteen of them and replaced them with one
sentence: refer to the instructions section of the NAVMC 10132 form itself.

Volume 15 is worse. Its posted text at paragraph 010502.B.1 reads "The Unit Punishment Book (UPB)
will not be used in officer NJP cases." MARADMIN 427/23 replaced that with the exact opposite:
"The Unit Punishment Book (UPB) will be used in officer NJP cases."

A clerk reading the posted Volume 15 today reaches a conclusion three years out of date, and it is
the conclusion a reasonable person would act on.

**Working rule: for UPB preparation, the form's own page 3 instructions govern. Not the MCO.**

### 0.2 The MCO's JAGMAN appendix letters no longer match the JAGMAN (CONFIRMED)

MCO 5800.16 Vol 14 para 010701 points a clerk to "reference (r), Appendices A-l-b, A-l-c, and
A-l-d" for the accused's rights. Reference (r) is JAGINST 5800.7G.

JAGINST 5800.7G Change Transmittal 2, dated 1 Dec 23, reissued the entire Appendix A-1 series,
pages A-1-a through A-1-q, and the lettering shifted at the front of the series. Under current
text:

| Letter | Was, under the older lettering | Is now, under 5800.7G CH-2 |
|---|---|---|
| A-1-a | rights-adjacent | Nonpunitive Letter of Caution sample |
| A-1-b | a rights form | **Letter of Instruction sample. Not a rights form** |
| A-1-c | rights form | Rights election, vessel exception DOES apply |
| A-1-d | rights form | Rights election, vessel exception does NOT apply |
| A-1-n | Article 31 rights advisory | **Request for Immediate Execution of Discharge** |
| A-1-o | - | **Suspect's Rights Acknowledgement/Statement** |

Two consequences. A clerk pulling "A-1-b" for rights, as the MCO instructs, pulls a Letter of
Instruction. And Marine Corps Judge Advocate Division Practice Advisory 21-4, of May 2021, names
"A-1-n" as the Article 31 rights advisory template, which was correct when written and is now the
discharge execution request.

There are two rights-election appendices now, not three. A-1-c and A-1-d, split solely on whether
the vessel exception applies.

### 0.3 The permission to use court-martial style charges was deleted (CONFIRMED)

Covered in section 1.4. It matters enough to flag here.

## 1. Before the UPB opens

### 1.1 How a case arrives

| Route | Document | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Military police or PMO | OPNAV 5580/1, Incident Report | REPORTED, MCO 5580.2B W/CH 2 |
| NCIS | Report of Investigation, the ROI. Felony-level referral per MARADMIN 209/04 | REPORTED |
| Chain of command | No standard form. Memo, verbal report, or unit log | REPORTED, unit practice |
| Urinalysis | Starts at DD Form 2624, see 1.6 | REPORTED, MCO 5300.17 |
| Civilian arrest | No standard intake document found | UNCONFIRMED |

### 1.2 The preliminary inquiry

R.C.M. 303 requires the immediate commander to make or cause to be made a preliminary inquiry on
receipt of information a member is suspected of an offense triable by court-martial.

The important finding for a clerk: **there is no mandatory paper trail.** The Naval Justice School
JAGMAN Investigations Handbook states there are no requirements or restrictions on how the
preliminary inquiry is accomplished, and the report need not be in writing, though some
documentation is advisable. JAGMAN Appendix A-2-c is a sample documentation format, a naval letter
layout, not a numbered form. JAGMAN 0201b directs that where the only basis is disciplinary
action, an R.C.M. 303 preliminary inquiry should be done rather than a separate JAGMAN
investigation. REPORTED.

### 1.3 Rights advisement during questioning

Before any person subject to the UCMJ questions a suspect, Article 31(b) rights are required. The
JAGMAN template is **Appendix A-1-o, Suspect's Rights Acknowledgement/Statement**, cross-referenced
at JAGMAN 0176. CONFIRMED from 5800.7G CH-2 text.

This is a different document, at a different time, from the NJP rights election in section 2. The
Article 31(b) waiver protects against self-incrimination during questioning, potentially weeks
before anyone decides on NJP.

Whether NCIS uses its own numbered rights certificate analogous to the Army's DA Form 3881 is
UNCONFIRMED. NCIS Manual 3 would settle it.

### 1.4 The charge sheet question, answered

**DD Form 458 is not a nonjudicial punishment document.** It is the Charge Sheet used to prefer
and refer charges for trial by court-martial under R.C.M. 307. Nothing in MCM Part V requires it
for NJP.

Marines calling the UPB or the notification a "charge sheet" are using loose shorthand.

The part worth knowing: MCO 5800.16 Vol 14 para 011105.A used to say "It is not necessary to use
model charges and specifications as in courts-martial, however, it is permissible to do so."
**MARADMIN 427/23 deleted that paragraph.** The NAVMC 10132 page 3 instruction replaced it, and
the replacement says nothing about charges or specifications. It says to enter the alleged
articles and offenses with date and place, to name the underlying offense for an attempt,
conspiracy, or solicitation, to specify the order for an orders violation, and to leave findings
blank until the hearing.

So the express permission is gone. Nothing prohibits specification-style drafting, and a unit
weighing NJP against court-martial has an obvious reason to draft that way, but the authority a
clerk would have cited for it is dead text. CONFIRMED against both the 2021 MCO and the form.

### 1.5 What the clerk pulls on the Marine

MCO 5800.16 Vol 14 para 011107 names the components in the appeal context, and they are the same
ones assembled for the hearing: applicable Page 11 entries, the Marine's Record of Service for
corporals and below, the UPB, the summarization of proceedings, and allied papers. Allied papers
are statements, investigative reports, documents, records, or photographs. CONFIRMED.

By name: Page 11 Administrative Remarks, NAVMC 118(11). Prior NAVMC 10132 entries. Basic
Individual Record, printed from MCTFS. The OMPF through Marine Online. Master Brief Sheet, though
whether it carries NJP history is UNCONFIRMED.

### 1.6 Urinalysis chain

DD Form 2624 Specimen Custody Document, prepared by the unit Urinalysis Program Coordinator. Then
the urinalysis ledger, the laboratory result through IFTDTL, a SACO drug testing determination
memo, and a command confirmation memo. REPORTED against MCO 5300.17 of 11 Apr 2011, which is
superseded by MCO 5300.17A W/ADMIN CH-2. **Reverify before relying on it.**

### 1.7 Legal review before imposition

None required. No provision in the JAGMAN or MCO 5800.16 Vol 14 requires a judge advocate to
review before NJP. Pre-imposition review is unit or installation SOP. The only mandatory judge
advocate review is post-imposition, on appeal, at Vol 14 para 011401. CONFIRMED.

## 2. Notification and election of rights

JAGMAN 0109 governs. The rights election form is **A-1-c** where the vessel exception applies and
**A-1-d** where it does not. The clerk prepares it, the accused reads, elects, and signs, and a
witness signs. CONFIRMED.

Under current 0109, the opportunity to consult defense counsel is extended to all cases when
operationally feasible, including vessel-exception cases. That is why the older three-form split
collapsed to two.

MCM Part V para 4 sets the content floor for the notice: a statement NJP is being considered, a
description of the alleged offense with the UCMJ article, a summary of or access to the supporting
evidence, a statement of rights, and where applicable the right to demand trial by court-martial.

**JAGMAN 0109 also requires a service record entry**, on NAVMC 118(11) for Marines, documenting
the counsel consultation opportunity and whether the accused used or waived it. Exact prescribed
wording is UNCONFIRMED. Two sources returned different strings. MCO P1070.12 would settle it.

Also at this stage, where applicable: **A-1-e, Acknowledgement of Advanced Education Assistance
Reimbursement**, for a Marine with an obligated service agreement.

## 3. The hearing, and the CO script

**JAGMAN Appendix A-1-f, "Nonjudicial Punishment Proceeding Guide."** This is the CO script.
CONFIRMED from 5800.7G CH-2 text, and JAGMAN 0110a calls it a guide for a personal appearance at
NJP proceedings.

It is a literal call-and-response script, CO to accused to witness, in this order: read the alleged
violations aloud with the no-obligation-to-speak warning, advise this is not a trial and the rules
of evidence do not apply, confirm the accused signed A-1-c or A-1-d and understood it, take the
plea, question witnesses and ask whether any adopt a prior written statement, confirm the accused
examined the documentary evidence, offer the chance to present a defense and matters in
mitigation, deliberate, announce findings and punishment, advise of the appeal right and timeline,
dismiss.

The script itself cross-references A-1-o: if the accused's statements at the hearing might later
be used at a court-martial, get the Article 31(b) waiver first.

**MCO 5800.16 Vol 14 para 011106** requires the officer imposing NJP to prepare, or cause to be
prepared, a summary transcript of the proceedings, using A-1-f or a locally prepared form, and it
is appended to the UPB. CONFIRMED. So A-1-f serves twice: script during, record after.

## 4. The UPB itself

Governed by the form's own page 3 instructions since MARADMIN 427/23. JAGMAN 0119a states the
forms used for the Unit Punishment Book are NAVPERS 1626/7 for Navy and NAVMC 10132 for Marine
Corps. CONFIRMED.

One upstream element lives inside the form. The page 3 instructions prescribe a required item 21
remark recording the company-level forwarding recommendation:

    YYYY-MM-DD ITEM 2: Fwd to Bn/Sqn CO recom [recommendation for court-martial, NJP,
    or specific NJP punishment].

So the company commander's recommendation up to the battalion or squadron CO is captured as a
remark rather than as a separate document. CONFIRMED from the form.

## 5. Running in parallel, the victim track

Two separate obligations, commonly conflated.

**VWAP notice.** Governed by MCO 5800.14, named directly in MARADMIN 427/23's reference line. One
SJA page names MCO 5800.16 instead, which reads as an error. REPORTED, flagged.

**DD Form 2701, Initial Information for Victims and Witnesses of Crime**, is the NJP-stage form,
triggered by initial contact rather than by a forum decision. DD 2702 is court-martial, DD 2703 is
post-trial, DD 2704 is confinement and prisoner status. None of the last three attach at NJP.
The unit has a Victim Witness Assistance Coordinator appointed in writing, the installation a
Victim Witness Liaison Officer. REPORTED.

**Right to be present, not a right to be heard.** MCO 5800.14 gives victims a right to be present
at NJP proceedings, subject to exclusion where a legal advisor finds clear and convincing evidence
the victim's testimony would be altered. Article 6b, UCMJ, the crime victims' rights statute, is
built around courts-martial and does not on its face extend to NJP. REPORTED, from SJA Practice
Advisory 15-3.

**Item 22 is not VWAP.** The demographic block exists to satisfy an FY22 NDAA requirement for the
Services to report race, ethnicity, and gender data in the military justice system to Congress
annually. It records status, sex, race, and ethnicity, and no name or identifier. The form's
instruction says to match the best entry from personnel records or the victim's self
identification, to enter "Unknown" if the victim declines, and to avoid deviations unless
necessary to avoid giving offense. CONFIRMED from the form.

## 6. Appeal

**5 days, and it is 5 days.** MCM Part V para 7.d, 2024 edition, verbatim: "An appeal shall be
submitted within 5 days of imposition of punishment, or the right to appeal shall be waived in the
absence of good cause shown." Para 7.c: "Appeals shall be in writing." CONFIRMED.

Do not carry "five working days" over from other contexts. A separate 5-day rule at para 7 gives a
stay: if action is not taken on the appeal within 5 days after submission, restriction and extra
duties are stayed on request. The form's own page 3 instruction confirms this with two prescribed
item 21 remarks reading "Appeal submitted d Mmm yy, five days elapsed with no action. Punishment
of restriction stayed."

**Package contents.** Vol 14 para 011107, CONFIRMED: applicable Page 11 entries, Record of Service
for corporals and below, the original UPB, the summarization of proceedings, and all allied
papers. JAGMAN 0116 independently requires the completed NAVMC 10132 as an enclosure to the
forwarding endorsement. JAGMAN 0117 sets appellate routing, with separate rules for the Navy and
Marine Corps chains.

The exact naval letter endorsement format is UNCONFIRMED.

**Judge advocate review before the appeal authority acts** is mandatory when the punishment
includes any of the seven categories at Vol 14 para 011402. CONFIRMED. Note 011402.G conditions
review on detention of pay, which MCM Part V does not authorize a commander to impose. That is
dead text and is finding 9 in the defect report.

After action, the appeal and all endorsements attach to the UPB, and the original returns to the
originating officer. Item 15 is completed by whoever notifies the accused of the decision.

## 7. Pay, personnel, and filing

**MCO 5800.16 Vol 14 para 011109, CONFIRMED verbatim:**

    The commanding officer will forward a copy of the UPB to the IPAC/Administrative section
    for the unit diary reporting and scanning in to the Marine's OMPF and ESR. The CO will
    deliver a copy to the Marine once proceedings are complete and final.

That single paragraph is the whole downstream chain: unit diary into MCTFS, OMPF, ESR, and the
Marine's copy. It survived MARADMIN 427/23, which deleted only the lettered subparagraphs of
011105.

Para 011110 requires the UPB be maintained in a binder or electronic file. Filing is under SSIC
5812, the code printed on the form. A specific NARA retention period is UNCONFIRMED. SECNAV
M-5210.1 would settle it.

Marine Corps specific MCTFS transaction codes for reduction, forfeiture, and restriction were not
located. The Navy MMPA FID code process is a different system and does not apply. UNCONFIRMED.

Handling of the UPB when an NJP is **set aside**, as opposed to vacated or mitigated, is
UNCONFIRMED. No primary source addressed it.

## 8. Suspension and vacation

Vol 14 paras 011201 and 011202 with Figure 14-1, the vacation letter. CONFIRMED.

A suspended punishment is vacated by any commander authorized to impose punishment of that kind
and amount, not necessarily the original imposing commander. Before vacating, the accused
ordinarily is notified and given an opportunity to respond using Figure 14-1. MCM Part V adds that
where the punishment is one of the more serious categories, the member should, unless
impracticable, be given an opportunity to appear.

Unit administrators then update block 16 of the original UPB and forward the vacation letter and
updated UPB to IPAC for unit diary reporting.

## 9. Officer cases, recorded and out of app scope

MARADMIN 427/23 amended MCO 5800.16 Vol 15 para 010502.B.1 to require the UPB in officer NJP
cases, added it at 010605.D as an enclosure, and modified Figure 15-5.

The officer package is not the UPB alone. It is the Report of Nonjudicial Punishment at Figure
15-5, built around notification at Figure 15-1 or 15-2, notice at Figure 15-3, and carrying the
UPB, an Acknowledgement of UPB Entry, and any matters the officer submits in response.

Routing timelines from the unamended Vol 15 text show 30 calendar days to forward and 20 calendar
days per intermediate endorser. Those figures are UNCONFIRMED against the amended order.
